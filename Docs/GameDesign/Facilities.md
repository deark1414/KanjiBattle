# Facilities

## MVP Facility Progression

Character unlock remains Research Lab driven. Its fixed unlock plan is synchronized with the stage encounter order and required cleared stages.

Facility | Role | Required Stage | Notes
---|---|---|---
研究所 | CharacterUnlock | 2 | Unlocks the next eligible character after its required stage has been cleared.
訓練場 | FormationSlot | 3 | Expands formation size through upgrades; unlock alone does not add a slot. Cap unlocks at stages 5, 12, 25, and 35 so effective slots reach 3/4/5/6 at the intended chapter beats.
城門 | ChapterUnlock | 5 | Opens chapters 2-8 through upgrades and cap unlocks at chapter gates.
召喚祭壇 | SummonCostDown | 2 | Keeps a larger summon pool affordable.
鍛冶屋 | UpgradeCostDown | 4 | Supports level progression.
金貨工房 | GoldProduction | 5 | Supports summoning and upgrades.
魔導書庫 | LevelCap | 10 | Opens higher character levels from chapter 3 onward. MVP target cap reaches 12.
勝利の碑 | StagePointBoost | 15 | Helps late chapter facility costs.
召喚加護 | SummonRateUp | Category chapter start | Biases summon odds after a category appears.
